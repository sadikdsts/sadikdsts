How is the % calculated?
CPI — Consumer Price Index. Currently 2.6%. This is the UK's official annual inflation measure — how much everyday goods & services cost over time. Use this to project non-salary costs like uni fees, holidays, or retirement spending.
AWE — Average Weekly Earnings. Currently 5.8%. Tracks how UK wages grow on average. Better for projecting salary-linked goals or costs that tend to rise with incomes.
Other — Enter your own rate (0–10%). Use this if you have a specific view on a particular cost or asset class, e.g. property inflation or education fees.
